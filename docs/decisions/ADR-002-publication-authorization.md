# ADR-002: Publication authorization

## Status

Accepted — 2026-09-15

## Context

The forensic review (`docs/reviews/2026-09-02-14fb9bd-forensic-review.md`) returned **ACCEPT** and found the study "public-ready pending separate publication authorization." No scientific, methodological, or evidentiary work remained open — the only gate was the publication decision itself, which is separate from and does not relax the evidence standard the review already applied.

## Decision

Joshua authorized publication on 2026-09-15. The repository (`SekiyaLab/spectral-recovery`) is made public on GitHub, and its `sekiyalab.github.io` research-index listing is updated from `Private` to `Public`.

## What did not change

No scientific content, results, methodology, review history, or accepted artifact was altered by this decision. The only content change is `README.md`'s "Review boundary" section, which stated public publication was not authorized — that statement is now stale and is corrected to record that it has occurred.

## Plain-language model

- **What this does:** turns an already-accepted, already-verified study from private to public.
- **Why it matters:** the study cleared its evidence bar on 2026-09-02; the estate held it privately gated for over a week on authorization alone, not on any remaining scientific question.
- **What fails if it is incorrect:** none of the science is affected — this is a visibility change, not a re-review.
- **What remains uncertain:** nothing about this study; general publication cadence for other accepted-but-private studies remains a separate future decision.
